Services

Two divisions, one delivery standard

Public sector construction organised the way our clients tender it — housing and social infrastructure, and roads and civil engineering — backed by a common project delivery capability.

Housing & Social Infrastructure

Division 01

BNG & RDP Housing

Turnkey construction of subsidised housing units for provincial Human Settlements programmes — from site establishment and foundations through to roofing, finishes and handover.

Building & Renovations

General building work, maintenance and renovation for public facilities and private clients alongside our core infrastructure projects.

Roads & Civil Engineering

Division 02

Road Construction & Upgrades

New road construction, resurfacing and upgrading of municipal and district roads, including earthworks, layer works and surfacing.

Stormwater & Civil Infrastructure

Stormwater drainage, culverts and general civil works that support housing and road developments.

Delivery Capability

What sits behind both divisions

Municipal & Government Contracts

End-to-end delivery of tendered municipal and provincial contracts, including project management, site supervision and progress reporting.

Site Supervision & Reporting
