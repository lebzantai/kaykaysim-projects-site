Verified from public tender and provincial oversight records — see full detail on our Projects page.

Free State Human Settlements — Serviced Sites

Appointed as one of three contractors delivering water and sewer installation, roads and stormwater across 895 serviced sites province-wide, as reported to Parliament's Human Settlements Portfolio Committee (June 2025). Target completion: 31 January 2027.

Dihlabeng Roads & Stormwater Panel

One of twelve contractors appointed to Dihlabeng Local Municipality's panel for construction and maintenance of roads and stormwater infrastructure on an as-and-when basis (2021–2024), including reconstruction of the 2km Nkitsing Bohlokong Road, Bethlehem (contract PW93/2021-BHM).
