Who We Are

A Free State contractor, focused on public infrastructure

KayKaysim Projects (Pty) Ltd is a civil and building construction company operating out of South Africa's Free State province, led by director Katleho Selhoane. We work primarily on tendered government and municipal contracts, including subsidised housing servicing (BNG/RDP), road construction, and related civil infrastructure work.

Our team combines site-level construction management with an understanding of the procurement, compliance and reporting requirements that come with public sector delivery — helping projects move from award to practical completion with fewer surprises along the way.

Our Approach

How we run a project

  1. 01 · Tender & Planning

    Scope, programme and resourcing are confirmed against the client's specification before any work starts on site.

  2. 02 · Mobilisation

    Site establishment, safety files, local labour engagement and material procurement are set up ahead of construction.

  3. 03 · Construction

    Work proceeds against the approved programme, with regular progress reporting and site supervision.

  4. 04 · Quality & Handover

    Snagging, quality checks and sign-off are completed with the client before practical completion and handover.

Track Record

Public sector work, on the public record

Rather than a generic project gallery, here's what's independently verifiable about our current government contracts.

Free State Human Settlements — Serviced Sites Programme

Appointed alongside two other contractors to deliver water and sewer installation, roads and stormwater across a province-wide rollout. KayKaysim's allocation covers 895 of the total serviced sites. Reported to Parliament's Portfolio Committee on Human Settlements following its oversight visit to the Free State on 11 June 2025; target completion 31 January 2027.

Dihlabeng Local Municipality — Roads & Stormwater Panel

Appointed to a panel of twelve contractors for construction and maintenance of roads and stormwater infrastructure on an as-and-when basis over a three-year term. Work under this panel included reconstruction of the 2km Nkitsing Bohlokong Road in Bethlehem (contract PW93/2021-BHM).
