Serviced Sites Programme Photo
Housing Infrastructure · Verified

Free State Human Settlements — Serviced Sites Programme

One of three contractors appointed to deliver water and sewer installation, roads and stormwater across a province-wide serviced sites rollout. KayKaysim's allocation covers 895 of the total sites (alongside KMC on 3,375 and Puno Harvest on 587). Confirmed to Parliament's Portfolio Committee on Human Settlements following its 11 June 2025 oversight visit to the Free State; target completion 31 January 2027.

Bohlokong Road Photo
Roads & Stormwater · Verified

Dihlabeng Roads & Stormwater Panel — Nkitsing Bohlokong Road

Appointed to Dihlabeng Local Municipality's panel of twelve contractors for construction and maintenance of roads and stormwater infrastructure on an as-and-when basis (three-year term from 2021). Panel work included reconstruction of the 2km Nkitsing Bohlokong Road, Bethlehem, under contract PW93/2021-BHM.
